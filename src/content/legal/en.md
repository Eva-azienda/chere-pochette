<h2 id="privacy">Privacy policy</h2>

We only process the data we need, in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). The controller is Chère Pochette (address above). Questions: [chere.pochette@sccbellinzona.ch](mailto:chere.pochette@sccbellinzona.ch).

### Visiting the website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). The server automatically logs technical data such as IP address, date, time and the page requested, to show the website and protect it from abuse. The automatic check of online payments also runs on GitHub. This data may therefore be processed in the United States; GitHub is certified under the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

We use no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. The fonts are hosted on our own website. When you press the button to buy or order, your browser only saves the language of the page (local storage, entry “cp-lang”) so that we can show you the confirmation in the same language: it contains no personal data and is not sent to us. You can refuse this by blocking local storage in your browser; the confirmation then appears in Italian.

The link to Instagram sends no data until you click it.

### Orders, payment and delivery

To handle an order we use your name, e-mail, phone number if given, delivery address, the bag you chose and the amount.

- Online payment takes place on Zahls.ch, a Swiss payment service provider: we never see your card or account details.
- If you order by e-mail, we use the details you send us only for the order and its delivery.
- For shipping, we pass your name and address to Swiss Post.

We keep accounting records for as long as the law requires; we delete all other data once we no longer need it.

### E-mail and newsletter

If you write to us, we use your data only to reply. Our e-mails go through the mail service of SCC Bellinzona.

If you sign up for the newsletter, we use your e-mail address only to send you our news. We keep the list ourselves, without any external service. You can unsubscribe at any time by writing to us.

### Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and withdraw a consent (for example for the newsletter). Write to [chere.pochette@sccbellinzona.ch](mailto:chere.pochette@sccbellinzona.ch). You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

<h2 id="terms">Terms of sale</h2>

### Our bags

Every bag is one of a kind, crocheted by hand from recovered materials. Small irregularities and slight colour differences from the photos are part of its character. Measurements are approximate.

### Prices

Prices are in Swiss francs (CHF) and are final prices. Shipping is a flat fee, shown on the website before you buy; pickup in Bellinzona is free.

### Ordering and payment

- **Online:** choose the bag and the delivery option, then pay on Zahls.ch (for example with TWINT, card or PostFinance). Before paying you can check and correct your details. The contract is concluded when the payment has gone through; you receive a confirmation by e-mail.
- **By e-mail** (while online payment is not active): you write to us from the bag's page. The contract is concluded when we confirm your order by e-mail; the confirmation tells you how to pay.

If, by mistake, the same bag is paid for twice, the second buyer receives a full refund.

### Delivery

We ship with Swiss Post within Switzerland and to Liechtenstein, usually within 5 working days of payment. If you choose pickup in Bellinzona, we contact you to arrange a time.

### Returns and defects

Swiss law gives no statutory right of withdrawal for online purchases, but we still accept returns: write to us within 14 days of receiving or collecting the bag. If it is unused and in its original condition, send it back (at your own cost) or hand it back in Bellinzona by appointment. We refund the price of the bag within 14 days of receiving it; the original shipping fee is not refunded.

If you live in Liechtenstein, the law gives you 14 days from receipt to withdraw from the contract without giving a reason: in that case we also refund the original shipping fee.

If the bag arrives damaged or faulty, write to us within 14 days with a photo: we will offer a repair or a refund. Normal wear is not a defect.

### Applicable law

Swiss law applies. The place of jurisdiction is Bellinzona, subject to the mandatory places of jurisdiction for consumers.
