### 1. Scope

These terms apply to all purchases made on the Chère Pochette website. By buying a bag, you accept them.

### 2. Our bags

Every bag is one of a kind, crocheted by hand from recovered materials.

Small irregularities and slight colour differences compared with the photos are part of its character and are not defects. The dimensions given are approximate.

### 3. Prices

Prices are in Swiss francs (CHF) and are final prices.

Shipping costs are shown on the website before payment and are added to the price of the bag. Pickup in Bellinzona is free.

### 4. Conclusion of the contract

The contract is concluded as soon as your payment on Zahls.ch has been completed successfully.

Each bag exists only once. If, because of a technical error, the same bag is paid for by two people, the second person receives a full refund.

### 5. Payment

Payment is made in advance via Zahls.ch, for example with TWINT, card or PostFinance. The available payment methods are shown on the payment page.

### 6. Delivery and pickup

We ship with Swiss Post within Switzerland and to Liechtenstein. Delivery times shown on the website are estimates.

If you choose pickup in Bellinzona, we will contact you after your purchase to arrange an appointment.

### 7. Returns (goodwill policy)

In Switzerland there is no statutory right of withdrawal for online purchases. We still offer you this option:

- write to us within 14 days of receiving or collecting your bag;
- the bag must be unused and in its original condition;
- return shipping is at your expense; alternatively, you can bring the bag back in Bellinzona by appointment;
- we refund the price of the bag within 14 days of receiving it back. The original shipping fee is not refundable.

### 8. Defects

Please check your bag when you receive it. If it is damaged or defective, write to us within 14 days and include a photo.

Every bag is unique and cannot be replaced with an identical one: we will offer you a repair or a refund. Normal wear and the typical features of recovered materials are not defects.

### 9. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, we are liable only for damage caused intentionally or through gross negligence.
